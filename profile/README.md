# 100 Ton Robot

Code and assets for public sharing.  Place to publish my projects I'd like to share with others.
It's named after our house.  Which I told my kid was a 100 ton robot covered with sensors.

## Projects

### [Dawn to Dusk Clock](https://github.com/100-Ton-Robot/dawn-to-dusk-clock-card)

A day/night world clock card for Home Assistant. It uses CSS gradients and the sun's position so you can see at a glance whether the sun is up in each location.

![Eight clocks from Auckland to San Francisco: night skies on the ends, daylight over Cairo, Paris and London, and two date bars (Thu, Nov 12 / Wed, Nov 11)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/100-Ton-Robot/dawn-to-dusk-clock-card/dev/images/screenshot.png)

### [BLE Beacon Tracker](https://github.com/100-Ton-Robot/ble-beacon-tracker)

A passive BLE beacon locator. Three nRF54L15 scanners behind directional antennas give a bearing and rough range, and an ESP32-S3 ESPHome gateway publishes the results to MQTT.
